## Allegations and interpretations that must remain labelled

- **Poisoning:** contemporary suspicion around Hamad’s death; not established here.
- **“Usurper”:** British and newspaper political label for Khalid; not a neutral identity.
- **“Puppet” or “British-installed”:** interpretive language about Hamoud’s position; use only with evidence and attribution.
- **“The British won in 38 minutes” / “Zanzibar lost”:** shorthand built from the outcome and a chosen clock; not the only defensible description.
- **“Bloodless”:** contradicted by reported Zanzibari casualties and should not be used.

## Not yet claimable

- Exactly 38 minutes as the sole or official duration.
- A 45-minute interval as an independently located source; the indexed later reconstruction gives 46 minutes, and any 45-minute rounding needs its own citation.
- Exactly 500 dead, or an exact civilian/defender split.
- Zero British casualties, two British casualties, or any final casualty count without the Admiralty and medical returns.
- That the 1890 succession clause gave Britain an unlimited legal right to choose the Sultan; inspect the agreement and legal context first.
- That Khalid personally poisoned Hamad.
- That Hamoud’s accession ended all meaningful Zanzibari political agency.
