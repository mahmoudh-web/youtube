## Classification key

- **Fact** — supported by a primary or official record.
- **Attributed position** — what a government, court, family, witness or later institution says.
- **Allegation** — a causal, negligent or intentional claim not established by the located record.
- **Association** — timing/location or epidemiological overlap without causal proof.
- **Interpretation** — analysis drawn from several claims.
- **Uncertainty** — unresolved discrepancy or missing evidence.

## Ledger stop rule

No row authorises final narration by itself. Human verification must inspect the original Special Report 142, full Wheat and Farmer articles, the district-court findings and the official hearing scan. Until then, the only safe causal language is “temporally associated,” “alleged,” “the Army argued,” or “not established.”
