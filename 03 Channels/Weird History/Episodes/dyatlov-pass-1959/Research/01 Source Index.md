## Research question

What does the surviving 1959 file actually establish about the group’s last camp, evacuation, deaths and investigation, and how far do the 2020 prosecutor review and the 2021 peer-reviewed avalanche model legitimately take the explanation?

## Source-handling rules

- A primary record is evidence of what was recorded, not automatically proof that every observation, translation or inference is correct.
- Keep the 1959 source wording, later prosecutor reconstruction and modern model in separate evidence categories.
- Every material claim belongs in `03 Claim Ledger.md` with a locator, confidence and caveat.
- Page numbers below refer to the case-file sheet numbers reproduced by the archive host. The English pages are translations/transcriptions; a reviewer should compare them with the linked Russian scans before narration.

## Key people and entities

- **Igor Dyatlov:** expedition leader; named in the route and closure records.
- **The nine deceased hikers:** Yuri Doroshenko, Lyudmila Dubinina, Igor Dyatlov, Aleksander Kolevatov, Zinaida Kolmogorova, Yuri Krivonischenko, Rustem Slobodin, Nikolay Thibeaux-Brignolle and Semyon Zolotaryov. Yuri Yudin began the expedition but returned before the fatal camp.
- **Vasily Tempalov and Lev Ivanov:** 1959 investigators named in the procedural records; their conclusions should be attributed to the file, not treated as omniscient reconstruction.
- **Andrey Kuryakov:** prosecutor associated with the 2018–20 review; statements are official-review findings as reported by S12–S14.
- **Johan Gaume and Alexander Puzrin:** authors of S15; their model tests a physical scenario, not the whole historical case.

## Research stop condition

No material claim is narration-ready until a second researcher checks the Russian scans, translator choices, geometry/weather assumptions, prosecutor source chain, and the paper’s limited claim of physical plausibility. No visual asset or script work is authorized by this pack.
