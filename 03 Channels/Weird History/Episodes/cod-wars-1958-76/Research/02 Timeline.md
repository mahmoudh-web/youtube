## Reading note

Dates below combine primary records and retrospective chronologies. “Established” means the date/event is supported by a primary or near-primary record; “attributed” means the event or detail is reported by a named source and still needs vessel-level or bilingual verification. The three confrontations overlap politically but are not one continuous battle.

## Timeline cautions

- The first wire-cut date, exact cutter mechanism, number of incidents, collisions, and casualty sequence require vessel logs.
- “War ended” can mean the end of each confrontation, the June 1976 access agreement, or the later normalization of the legal order. Use the phase-specific date.
- Icelandic sources may use local terms and dates differently in translation; a bilingual review is required.
