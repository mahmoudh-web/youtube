## Claims not cleared for narration

- “The Cuyahoga River fire created the EPA.” The legal record shows a broader reorganization process.
- “The fire caused the Clean Water Act.” Use contributory language only.
- “The fire was the worst ever on the river,” “the only burning river,” or “the first river to burn.” These are contradicted by the record.
- “The train definitely dropped a flare,” unless a primary incident record is found.
- “Time published a photograph of the 1969 fire.” The located fact-check says the image was from 1952.
- “No one was harmed,” “there were no casualties,” or “the fire was harmless.” The current pack has no comprehensive injury audit and does not support minimizing the pollution or bridge damage.
- Exact damage totals, exact fire height and exact number of prior fires without scan-level reconciliation.
- “Cleveland officials or industry intentionally staged the fire for publicity.” No source supports that motive.
- “The river was restored completely.” EPA’s current Area of Concern page lists ongoing impairments and remediation.
