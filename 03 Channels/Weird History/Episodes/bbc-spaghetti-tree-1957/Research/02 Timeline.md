## Safe chronology

The defensible sequence is: a *Panorama* team developed and filmed a short fictional harvest report in the Ticino/Lake Lugano area in March 1957; BBC Programme Index records the *Panorama* broadcast at 20:30 on 1 April 1957; the surviving film and narration presented the fiction in a serious report style; contemporary and retrospective accounts record immediate telephone and later written responses; the exact distribution of belief remains unknown.

## Chronology gaps for validation

- Exact production dates, locations, participants and budget paperwork.

- Original 2 April 1957 newspaper issue and BBC spokesman source.
- Telephone exchange logs, letters, internal memos and any audience research.
- Stable copies of BBC’s 2004 and 2014 retrospective interviews.
