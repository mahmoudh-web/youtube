## Legal, ethical and wording controls

- No criminal allegation or individual misconduct finding is being asserted in this pack.
- “Accident,” “mishap,” “fuel-vapour explosion” and “one fatality” are safer than “nuclear disaster narrowly averted” unless the counterfactual is explicitly identified as speculation.
- The deceased and injured service members are historical subjects, not plot devices. Confirm names, ranks, dates and medical descriptions from primary records before use.
- A source saying “nuclear weapon accident” does not mean a nuclear detonation or radioactive release occurred.
