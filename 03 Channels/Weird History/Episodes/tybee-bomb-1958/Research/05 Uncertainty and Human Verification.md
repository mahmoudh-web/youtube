3. **Mod 0 conversion record:** Does the Air Force assessment’s maintenance and AEC production-record reasoning have attached citations or release numbers that can be independently checked?
4. **Time discrepancy:** Why do S1’s background section and S2/S5 give about 2:00 versus 3:30 a.m.? Resolve against the accident report, mission log and contemporary newspaper record.
5. **Search record:** Can the 6 February–16 April 1958 search logs establish the exact area, equipment, weather/tide limitations, and formal wording of the termination decision?
6. **Impact point:** Is any coordinate more precise than “Wassaw Sound area” supported by a primary record? If not, keep all maps approximate and clearly labelled.
7. **2001 modeling:** Are the 5–15-foot burial estimate and conventional-explosive risk based on assumptions that remain valid? They are historical estimates, not current hazard advice.
8. **2004–05 survey:** Locate the complete interagency/laboratory report behind S3; determine what the 12 seabed samples can and cannot exclude outside the sampled area.
9. **Later status:** Has any authoritative agency changed the “irretrievably lost” status after the 2005 release? No such change was located in this pass, but that is not proof that none exists.

## Language and translation controls

- Use **“Mk 15 Mod 0 bomb/nuclear weapon”** when describing the object, not **“armed hydrogen bomb,”** unless the original Howard document is verified and reconciled.
- Use **“jettisoned,” “released” or “lost”** rather than “dropped” when describing the deliberate emergency release; “dropped” can imply an ordinary planned bomb run.
- Use **“Wassaw Sound area off Tybee Beach”** unless a primary record supports a narrower location.
- Explain that **“no nuclear capsule”** is the official Air Force/DOE configuration conclusion; do not imply that no radioactive or conventional material was present.
- Keep **nuclear detonation**, **conventional-explosive hazard**, **heavy-metal contamination**, and **radiation readings** as separate technical questions.
- Quote **“irretrievably lost”** with attribution and explanation. It is a status label, not a proof of physical destruction or continued presence.

## Legal, ethical and editorial-risk flags
