## Safe wording gate

> On 1 April 1957, BBC *Panorama* aired a short fictional report from Switzerland in which people appeared to harvest spaghetti from trees. The report borrowed the authority of a serious current-affairs programme, documentary images and Richard Dimbleby’s deadpan narration. Contemporary and later accounts say hundreds of viewers contacted the BBC, some asking whether the story was true or how to grow their own spaghetti. The record shows a striking response, but it does not tell us what percentage of the audience believed the claim.

This wording deliberately leaves out the unverified audience total, exact call count, exact filming site, and any claim that Britain as a whole was fooled.
