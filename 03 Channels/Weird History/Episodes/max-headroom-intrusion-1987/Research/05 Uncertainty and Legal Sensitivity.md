## Fact / allegation / interpretation / uncertainty controls

- **Fact:** the located sources record two interruptions, their broad sequence, the visible performance and WGN’s reported recovery action.
- **Allegation or attribution:** engineers’ descriptions of required expertise, the FCC’s reported search, and any theory about an insider or motive must stay attached to their source.
- **Interpretation:** the differing station recoveries may expose different control points or operational vulnerabilities; this is a hypothesis, not a reconstructed attack.
- **Uncertainty:** the identity, number of participants, exact RF path, equipment, motive, full FCC/FBI record and legal status remain open.

Do not turn “the FBI FOIA log lists a request” into “the FBI investigated and failed.” Do not turn “a WTTW engineer thought sophisticated knowledge was needed” into “the culprit was a broadcast engineer.” Do not turn “no responsive documents” into “no records ever existed.”

## Legal, ethical and editorial sensitivity

### Defamation and false attribution

This is an unresolved crime-like public mystery. Naming an uncharged person as the performer, collaborator or organiser could be defamatory even when the person is a historical or internet-discussed figure. Do not publish suspect names, home locations, workplaces or family details without a documented, independently corroborated basis and legal review. “No public identification located” is safer than implying guilt from technical background or geographic proximity.

### Criminal and legal claims

The 1987 reporting uses “video piracy” and quotes possible penalties, but the pack does not establish the exact offence charged or applicable limitations period. Do not state that the case became legally impossible to prosecute, that a specific penalty applied, or that the investigation ended for legal reasons without primary legal authority and counsel review.

### Graphic and sexual material

The WTTW segment includes partial nudity and sexualised parody. It is relevant to identifying the surviving broadcast, but repeated description, stills or clips could be gratuitous or unsuitable for some audiences. Human editorial review must decide whether any non-graphic reference is necessary and whether any archive excerpt is permitted.

### Privacy and personal data

The names of station staff and engineers appear in public professional reporting. Do not add private contact details, addresses, family information or unverified biographical claims. Do not use online forum handles as identity evidence.

### Copyright and archival rights

WTTW’s web pages and Archive.org copies establish access and discovery, not permission to reuse. The original broadcast may involve station, programme, music, performer and recording rights. A human must identify the rights holder(s), obtain written permission or make a documented fair-use decision with legal review, and preserve attribution/territorial restrictions.
