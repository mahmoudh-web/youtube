# Psychology

**Status:** Unvalidated hypothesis

## Provisional promise

Psychological ideas and behaviours explained for an international general audience.

## Failure points

- Pop-psychology claims may be presented as settled science.
- Mental-health topics may cause harm if oversimplified.
- Studies may be misrepresented or poorly replicated.
- The niche is crowded and may lack a distinct promise.
