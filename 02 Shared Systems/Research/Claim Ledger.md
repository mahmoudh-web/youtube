## Language controls

- “The regulator alleged…”
- “The company agreed to settle…”
- “The court found…”
- “The jury convicted…”
- “The judgment was later appealed…”

Use “fraud,” “criminal,” “guilty,” or similar terms only when the legal record supports them.
