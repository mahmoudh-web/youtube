## Timeline reading note

The timeline has three separate clocks: (1) fishing incidents and seizures, (2) the February–March 1963 naval signalling crisis, and (3) diplomatic/legal settlement continuing into 1964. The sources do not justify compressing all three into a single “war day” or one uncontested withdrawal date.
