## Causal sequence under review

The evidence supports this sequence: long-running industrial and municipal pollution → pre-existing activism and policy pressure → modest 22 June fire → Stokes/Klaric local reframing → Time’s national image/text → broader environmental mobilisation and legislative momentum → EPA/CWA within a multi-cause policy chain. The fire is best described as a vivid catalyst and symbol, not a lone legal cause.
