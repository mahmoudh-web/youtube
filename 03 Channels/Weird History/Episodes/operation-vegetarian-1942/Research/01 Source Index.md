## Research question

What does the public record establish about how Britain authorised and tested Operation Vegetarian, why the cattle-feed plan was not deployed, and what official records say about Gruinard Island’s later decontamination and return to civil ownership?

## Episode promise and scope

The defensible story is not “Britain secretly poisoned Germany.” It is that Britain prepared a retaliatory biological-weapons capability, tested related anthrax delivery concepts on a Scottish island, never used the cattle-feed plan against Germany, and then spent decades managing the consequences of the test site. The pack does not reconstruct weapon operation or make a counterfactual casualty estimate.

## Source-quality key

- **A — official/primary record:** government statement, Hansard answer, official catalogue or contemporary institutional record.
- **B — official institutional synthesis:** Dstl, IWM, PBS or government research guidance summarising records.
- **C — specialist or peer-reviewed secondary analysis:** scholarly review used to triangulate and identify caveats.
- **D — reputable reporting/public reproduction:** journalism reporting released files or quoting experts; useful but not a substitute for the underlying archive.
- **E — lead only:** search result or access route that requires human inspection before material claims are made.

## Source-handling rules

1. Use S5 and S7 to map authorisation, but label the chronology as secondary until the original memo, Defence Committee minutes and War Cabinet/Porton files are inspected.
2. Use S1–S3 for the institutional fact of Gruinard testing. Do not turn the IWM film description into a graphic sequence or an operational explainer.
3. Use S8–S12 for the decontamination sequence: review, field work, advisory assessment, agricultural-use demonstration and public confirmation of safety.
4. Use S4 and S7 to distinguish “retaliatory rationale” from proof that Germany had deployed a biological weapon. The review explicitly says evidence for German FMD weapons was limited.
5. Treat “five million” as a reported production figure requiring human confirmation against the records; do not include recipes, doses, filling methods or delivery specifications.
6. No visual asset is approved for production by this research pack.
