## Chronology guardrails

- Do not state that Australia formally declared war on emus. The located official record describes an expedition/cull and a machine-gun deployment; “war” appears as press language.
- Do not collapse the 8 Nov “Central Australia” question into the Western Australian operation. The 9 Nov answer is the source that identifies the wheat-belt soldier settlers.
- Do not set a precise end date or exact kill total until the underlying Defence paperwork and contemporary reports are reconciled.
- Keep “initial withdrawal/return of guns” and “later resumed operation” as distinct phases. A one-line “the army went in and immediately lost” narrative erases the second phase and the administrative ambiguity.
