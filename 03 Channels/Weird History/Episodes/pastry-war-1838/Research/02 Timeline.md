## Reading guide

Dates are separated from interpretation. “Contemporary record” means an event or official position appears in a document produced during the dispute. “Later account” means a retrospective narrative or modern synthesis. The pastry incident and the exact French claim schedule remain unresolved; the blockade, military action and final convention are much better documented.

## Timeline conclusions

1. The documented escalation runs from unresolved diplomatic and commercial claims to ultimatum, blockade, naval attack and negotiated settlement.
2. The pastry grievance is present in Mexican archival and later scholarly narratives, but its amount, wording, incident details and causal weight are not established by the 1839 treaty text or the SRE summary alone.
3. The 600,000 pesos was a negotiated aggregate for pre-26-November French claims. It should not be narrated as “France demanded 600,000 pesos for cakes.”
