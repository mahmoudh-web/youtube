# Research Fraud — Failure Points

- Technical complexity may make the videos inaccessible.
- A retraction may be mistaken for proof of intentional fraud.
- The channel may run out of visually compelling cases.
- Scientific uncertainty may be flattened into a simple villain story.
- Defamation and reputational risk still apply.

## Control

Distinguish error, negligence, misconduct, and fraud; use primary papers and institutional findings; and have technically capable reviewers check explanations.
