# False Verdicts — Failure Points

- The legal record may be more complicated than the narrative.
- Appeals or later findings may be missed.
- Simplification may unfairly affect living people or victims.
- The channel may become sensational rather than explanatory.
- A weak evidence trail can undermine the entire episode.

## Control

Require primary legal records, an appeal/status check, careful language, and an editorial review focused on fairness and uncertainty.
