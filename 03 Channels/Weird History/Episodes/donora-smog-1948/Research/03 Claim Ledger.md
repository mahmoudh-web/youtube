## Wording gate

The current safest headline fact is: **“The 1949 Public Health Service report counted 20 deaths and 5,910 people affected in the Donora area, and concluded that the disaster resulted from accumulated pollution under extreme atmospheric stability—not one proven chemical or one legally adjudicated culprit.”** This wording remains provisional until S2 and the Pennsylvania state files are inspected.
