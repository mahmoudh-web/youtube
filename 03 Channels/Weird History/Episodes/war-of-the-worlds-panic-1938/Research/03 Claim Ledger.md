## Wording gate

Current wording that may advance only after human verification:

> On 30 October 1938, CBS’s *Mercury Theatre on the Air* presented an adaptation of H. G. Wells’s *The War of the Worlds* as a sequence of realistic news bulletins. Some listeners mistook parts of the drama for real news, and letters, FCC records and local reports document genuine alarm. But the familiar story of a nation-wide panic is not a single settled measurement: contemporary headlines, Cantril’s later estimate, surviving listener correspondence and later methodological criticism describe different kinds of evidence. The defensible question is not whether anyone panicked, but how a real and uneven reaction became a national legend.

This wording intentionally omits a settled national headcount, universal belief claim, verified nationwide flight, a proven newspaper conspiracy and any statement that the entire reaction was invented.
