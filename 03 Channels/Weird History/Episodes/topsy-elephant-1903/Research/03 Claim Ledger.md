- Whether the utility was formally “Brooklyn Edison,” the precise corporate name in January 1903, and the exact spelling/identity of P. D. Sharkley/Sharkey.
- Whether cyanide, electrocution or strangulation was the physiological cause of death; the newspapers do not settle the sequence.
- Whether any surviving Edison Manufacturing production order, ledger, camera log or correspondence explains why the crew filmed Topsy.
