## Chronology controls

- Use **5 February 1958** for the collision/jettison and **16 April 1958** for the official search termination.
- Preserve the exact-time discrepancy (**about 2:00 a.m.** in S1 versus **3:30 a.m.** in S2/S5) until the accident record is checked.
- Keep the 1966 “complete weapon” entry in a separate evidentiary lane from the 2001/2005 Air Force/DOE technical conclusion.
- Never turn a conditional 5–15-foot burial estimate into proof that the weapon is still there.
