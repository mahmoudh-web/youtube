## Wording gate

Current wording that may advance only after human verification:

> On 8 November 1861, USS *San Jacinto* stopped the British mail steamer *Trent* and removed Confederate commissioners James Mason and John Slidell, who were travelling to seek European recognition. The United States celebrated the capture; Britain argued that a neutral vessel and flag had been violated. The resulting crisis mixed rival interpretations of maritime law with military preparations around Canada, public and financial pressure, and the Union’s need to avoid a second war. Seward’s December response released the prisoners while preserving an American legal argument; Britain accepted the release, their return to British protection and the accompanying explanation as sufficient reparation. The record supports a grave diplomatic crisis that risked armed conflict, not a war that actually occurred.

This wording intentionally omits a definitive legal verdict, a single troop total, a claim that Lincoln acted alone, a claim that Seward formally apologised, and any statement that Britain had already decided to declare war.
