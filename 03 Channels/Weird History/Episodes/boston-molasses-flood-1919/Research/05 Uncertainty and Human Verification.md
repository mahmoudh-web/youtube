## Legal and ethical sensitivity

- The sabotage allegation concerns anarchism and Boston’s Italian immigrant community. Do not use group identity as causal shorthand; distinguish the company’s allegation from evidence and finding.
- “Negligence” and “liability” are civil/legal terms. Do not upgrade them to “criminal,” “fraud,” “murder” or “conviction.”
- The event killed workers, children and a firefighter. Do not use graphic casualty descriptions for spectacle; human review should approve any archival image or quotation involving bodies or severe injury.
- The case record includes personal death, injury and family-compensation details. Use only details that materially serve the historical question and that are verified against the official record.
- Avoid claiming that the disaster alone created all modern engineering regulation. Verify statutes, ordinances and dates before making a legacy claim.
