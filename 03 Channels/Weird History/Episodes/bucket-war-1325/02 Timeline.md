## Chronology guardrails

- Use **1325** as the secure year. Use **15 November** as the commonly published modern date, but have a human check the critical Griffoni and relevant Modena chronicle editions before treating the day as primary-level certainty.
- Keep **Fossalta (1249)** separate from **Zappolino (1325)**. Tassoni’s poem deliberately folds material from both conflicts into a comic architecture.
- The bucket’s reported removal belongs, at safest, to the campaign’s **later memory as a trophy**. The sources located do not prove that the bucket theft preceded the war or caused it.
- A 1326 peace is useful context, but the treaty’s exact terms and what happened to castles, prisoners, and borders require verification from the diplomatic record.
