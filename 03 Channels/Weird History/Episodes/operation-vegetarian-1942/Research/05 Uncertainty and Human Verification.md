## Fact / allegation / interpretation / uncertainty controls

- **Fact:** Public institutional sources record Porton Down biological-warfare research, Gruinard testing, non-deployment of Operation Vegetarian and later decontamination/safety confirmation.
- **Allegation or source-attributed rationale:** The plan is described in later sources as a retaliatory response to suspected German biological activity; evidence of German FMD weapons is described as limited.
- **Interpretation:** The episode is best understood as a chain of escalation, testing, non-use and long-tail remediation rather than a secret attack that happened in Germany.
- **Uncertainty:** The exact wartime approval/cancellation documents, the scope of the cattle-cake stockpile and the complete scientific basis for the 1990 safety declaration remain to be checked.

## Biological-weapons sensitivity rules

1. Do not describe cultivation, amplification, filling, weapon assembly, delivery geometry, target selection, dose, strain, persistence engineering or attack optimisation.
2. Do not reproduce procedural details from the IWM film, archival reports or technical papers. Cite them as records and summarise only their historical role.
3. Do not provide instructions for decontamination. The pack may say that official records describe liquid remediation and follow-up verification, but it must not provide concentrations, application rates, equipment or step sequences.
4. Do not use graphic footage, animal-death imagery, pathogen close-ups or faux operational documents.
5. Do not state or imply that Operation Vegetarian was used in Germany, that it caused human deaths, or that a counterfactual death toll is historically settled.
6. Any script, visual prompt, thumbnail, narration or title must pass a human editorial safety review before production.

## Legal, ethical and editorial sensitivity

### Historical harm and accountability

The main actors are historical, but the subject concerns state violence, animal experimentation, biological weapons and a long-contaminated landscape. Avoid “mad scientists,” “genius plan,” “secret weapon that nearly won the war” and similar glamorisation. The evidence supports institutional secrecy and a serious wartime programme; it does not establish personal motives beyond the records.

### Animal welfare and graphic treatment

The IWM catalogue describes animal trials, but the research pack must not turn animal suffering into spectacle. Any later visual use would need provenance, rights, contextual captioning and a non-graphic alternative.

### Public safety and misinformation

Do not turn the 1990 safety declaration into a present-day access recommendation. Do not publish a map or route that could be read as an invitation to disturb a historical site. The pack is historical documentation, not public-health or site-safety advice.

### Copyright and archival use

Public access to Hansard, Dstl, IWM or archival pages is not blanket permission to reuse images or film. Human review must record rights, licence, exact caption, date, provenance and whether the asset contains graphic or sensitive material.

## Human-verification stop gate

Research stops here. Before any script or production material is made, a human editor should:

1. inspect the original 1941 memo and January 1942 Defence Committee record behind the PBS chronology;
2. locate and read the released War Cabinet/Porton experiments sub-committee files, separating primary wording from the Guardian’s report;
3. verify the five-million production figure, storage and disposal records;
4. determine whether a formal cancellation/abandonment record exists and separate it from the later anthrax-bomb programme;
5. inspect the Gruinard trial and decontamination records at a non-graphic, non-operational level;
6. verify the independent advisory group’s 1987–90 evidence and the precise public safety announcement;
7. approve all fact/allegation/interpretation/uncertainty labels and biological-weapons sensitivity controls;
8. approve rights, dignity and safety treatment for any visual source.

This pack is **not narration-ready** and contains no script, title test, thumbnail brief, visual-generation prompt, edit plan or publication asset.
