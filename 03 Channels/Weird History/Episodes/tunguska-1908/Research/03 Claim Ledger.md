## Provisional wording gate

> On 30 June 1908, a powerful atmospheric explosion struck the Siberian taiga near the Podkamennaya Tunguska River. Witnesses described a luminous body, heat, sound and violent air movement; the first successful scientific expedition, led by Leonid Kulik in 1927, found a vast radial forestfall but no ordinary crater or proven large fragment. Modern models favour a stony asteroid that broke apart around 10 km above the ground, while the exact composition—and claims about Lake Cheko—remain unresolved.

This wording is research-only. It deliberately avoids “meteorite hit the ground,” a precise body size as fact, a definitive comet/asteroid identification, the claim that all witnesses saw the same thing, and exotic theories as if they were live equals.
