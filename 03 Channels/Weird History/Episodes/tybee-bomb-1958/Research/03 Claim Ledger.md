## Status key

- **Established:** directly supported by an official/primary record located in this pass.
- **Attributed:** a source or institution makes the claim; wording must name that source or viewpoint.
- **Interpretation:** an inference that requires explicit qualification.
- **Open:** not safe to state until the required primary check is complete.

## Wording gate

The only currently safe core proposition is: **“Official records establish that a B-47 jettisoned a Mk 15 Mod 0 bomb into the Wassaw Sound area after a 1958 collision; the original search failed to locate it, and later Air Force/DOE reviews kept it classified as irretrievably lost while concluding the training-configuration weapon had no nuclear capsule. A reported 1966 congressional record creates an unresolved configuration dispute that must be checked against the original documents.”**
