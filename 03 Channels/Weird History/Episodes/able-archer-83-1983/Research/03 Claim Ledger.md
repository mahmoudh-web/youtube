## Claims not cleared for narration

- “The Soviets were seconds/minutes/hours from launching.” No source in this pack establishes a countdown or launch authority being activated.
- “The Politburo ordered a preemptive nuclear strike.” The PFIAB language is qualified and the Soviet leadership response is not fully documented.
- “Able Archer was a secret NATO plan to launch a first strike.” The exercise was an announced annual command-post exercise; the question is how it was interpreted, not what NATO secretly intended.
- “Reagan, Weinberger or national leaders personally participated in the exercise.” SHAPE explicitly rejects this; small U.S. and UK response cells simulated political authorities.
- “The exercise ended early because Moscow backed down.” SHAPE says it ended on its long-planned schedule.
- “The CIA missed everything.” Contemporaneous intelligence reported increased alert; the problem was fragmentation, interpretation and lack of context.
- “RYaN caused Able Archer.” The program and the exercise coexisted in a tense system, but direct causal proof is disputed.
- “Gordievsky alone saved the world.” This assigns an unprovable counterfactual and collapses British, U.S., NATO and Soviet evidence into a hero narrative.
