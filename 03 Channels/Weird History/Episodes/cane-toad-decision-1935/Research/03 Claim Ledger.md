## Unusable or unsafe shorthand

- “No evidence” → replace with “no adequate Australian pre-release test or environmental assessment has been located in this pack.”
- “The Queensland government released the toads” → identify BSES and Mungomery, then explain the unresolved higher approval chain.
- “Froggatt warned them before release” → the warning record must be dated; the first release preceded the cited 1936 publication.
- “The toads ate no beetles” → later sources report some target-beetle feeding but limited control.
- “The toads killed Australia’s wildlife” → specify the toxic-ingestion pathway and affected taxa; preserve variation and counterevidence.
