Confidence describes how safely a claim can be stated with its source and qualifier attached. It does not mean every causal interpretation is settled. “Open caveat” travels with the claim into later editorial work.

## Not yet claimable

- “A 1,720-foot wave” without the words **run-up elevation**, **trimline elevation** or an equivalent clarification.
- A precise open-water crest height inferred directly from the 1,720-ft trimline.
- A single definitive landslide volume, slide count or source geometry without attributing the model/study.
- “The wave was 524 m high” — the 524 m figure is a maximum run-up elevation above sea level.
- A coast-wide tsunami of 1,700 ft, an open-ocean wall of water, or a direct eyewitness view of water reaching the 1,720-ft contour.
