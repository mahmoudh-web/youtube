## Wording gate

Current wording that can advance only after human verification:

> On 4–5 May 1945, a small, improvised defense at Castle Itter brought U.S. soldiers together with Wehrmacht personnel tied to the Wörgl resistance, local Austrian resistance members and some French detainees against an attacking Waffen-SS force. A U.S. unit report places the main attack at about 10:00, the heavy firefight until about 14:00 and relief at about 15:00; other accounts place relief around 16:00. Major Sepp Gangl was fatally shot. This episode shows an individual German military contingent opposing the SS in one emergency—it does not make the Wehrmacht a clean institution.

That wording intentionally omits a settled attacker strength, exact prisoner count, Gangl’s precise death mechanism, a universal “only battle” claim and any unsupported execution order.
