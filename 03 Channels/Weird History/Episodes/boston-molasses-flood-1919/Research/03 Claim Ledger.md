## Legal wording control

- **Allegation:** USIA’s sabotage/anarchist theory.
- **Criminal procedure:** Bolster’s inquest and the grand jury’s no-indictment result.
- **Civil evidence:** testimony and expert reports heard in the consolidated Suffolk County proceeding.
- **Auditor finding:** Ogden’s report assigning liability to USIA and rejecting sabotage.
- **Outcome:** later settlement; no final jury verdict established in this pack.
