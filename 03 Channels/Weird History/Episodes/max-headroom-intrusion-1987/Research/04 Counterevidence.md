## Purpose

This note tests the strongest story-shaped claims against contrary, weaker or missing evidence. It is intended to prevent a clean “inside hacker with a giant transmitter and a clear motive” narrative from outrunning the record.

## Technical reconstruction boundary

The narrowest supportable technical description is:

1. Contemporary station engineers described microwave links carrying programme feeds from studios to downtown transmitters.
2. A WGN engineer reportedly changed the link frequency and recovered the normal feed.
3. WTTW’s later account says its interruption continued for about 90 seconds, after which the pirate signal disappeared and normal programming returned.
4. Those facts are consistent with an attack on a studio-to-transmitter path, but they do not establish the transmitter power, antenna, modulation, launch site, line-of-sight geometry, exact frequency, or whether the same setup was used twice.

Any diagram or calculation would be a later technical reconstruction and would need an engineer’s review. It must not be presented as a recovered FCC finding.

## Identity and motive boundary

No public source located for this pack identifies the masked performer(s). The references in the performance are evidence of what was broadcast, not a confession, signature or reliable geographic marker. Any theory naming an uncharged person should be treated as an allegation requiring extraordinary corroboration and is excluded from this research record.
