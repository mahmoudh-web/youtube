## Unsafe shorthand

- “Britain poisoned Germany” → “Britain prepared, but did not deploy, a plan to contaminate cattle feed.”
- “Churchill ordered Operation Vegetarian” → “A PBS chronology reports a Defence Committee go-ahead in January 1942; the underlying minute still requires inspection.”
- “Gruinard was cleaned in 1986” → “Dstl dates the main decontamination to 1986; official records show advisory review and public safety confirmation followed.”
- “It would have killed millions” → exclude; this is an unsupported counterfactual.
- “All anthrax work happened on Gruinard” → false; Hansard records a separate 1942 Penclawdd test and Porton Down work.
