## Causal sequence to preserve

The strongest defensible sequence is: crop-damage framing and public scientific advice → central Four Pests directive → mass local campaigns and official success reporting → scientific reassessment and archival policy discussion → March/April 1960 substitution of bedbugs → famine period in which multiple institutional, political, environmental and ecological mechanisms interacted.

The sequence does **not** establish: “sparrows were killed, therefore the famine happened.” It establishes a policy intervention whose ecological effects and relative contribution to food losses require separate evidence.
