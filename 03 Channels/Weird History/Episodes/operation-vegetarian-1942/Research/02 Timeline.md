## Sequence answer

The record supports a four-stage sequence: (1) institutional authorisation to produce a retaliatory capability, (2) high-level field testing on Gruinard, (3) preparation without deployment and later destruction of the stockpile, and (4) a separate post-war remediation and ownership process. It does not support a single cinematic “Churchill ordered an attack, then cancelled it” account without the underlying minutes.
