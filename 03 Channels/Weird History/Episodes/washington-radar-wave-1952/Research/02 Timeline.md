## Reading rule

This timeline separates reported observation, official action and later explanation. “Unidentified” means that the witness or controller did not identify the return/object at the time; it does not mean extraterrestrial. The source records use EST, EDT and report-time notation inconsistently; no single normalized clock should be used until the original pages are reconciled.

## Source-level time policy

The current record contains at least three time conventions: S2’s case narrative uses EDT; S5’s CAA Table I uses EST; S9 converts AFOSI material to EDT and labels the conversion. S3 and S4 are image scans whose first-weekend notations require manual reading. The table below preserves those source conventions instead of silently merging them.

## Timeline gaps and source-level conflicts

- Original Washington National and Andrews operations logs for both weekends are not yet linked with stable page/image identifiers.
- The Blue Book case file says the two interceptor flights’ official reports had not been received and that several commercial/CAA pilots had not been interviewed.
- S3/S4, S5 Table I and S9 use different time conventions and sometimes different event labels. No canonical conversion should be published until the original images are manually aligned.
- NARA catalogue metadata identifies the relevant case-file and Air Intelligence series, but no item-level F-94 mission report or complete pilot file was located in the accessible catalogue during this revision.
- The 29 July press transcript remains a secondary transcription; NARA catalogue record 25738 confirms the 31 July film record, but its full audiovisual content was not independently reviewed here.
- Newspaper chronology should be rebuilt from original 1952 issues, not only retrospective quotations, the S2 clipping index or secondary reproductions.
