## Research question

What does the geological, chemical, medical and eyewitness record establish about the Lake Nyos disaster, and what remains disputed about the mechanism that released the gas?

## Conflict and provisional episode promise

The event looked like an explosion but killed without the normal signatures of fire, blast or a conventional flood. The strongest located evidence supports a sudden release of magmatic CO₂ that had accumulated in the deep, chemically stratified lake; the unresolved question is what initiated the release. The Italian technical mission attributed the outburst to a phreatic explosion beneath the lake, while the USGS/limnological interpretation found no evidence of direct volcanic activity and did not identify a single trigger. This pack preserves both positions and does not convert the leading interpretation into certainty.

## Source-quality key

- **A — primary/official or original study:** USGS report, official Cameroon ministry document, Smithsonian event record, peer-reviewed original research or PubMed record for the original medical study.
- **B — contemporaneous or institutional synthesis:** early event reporting, official scientific summaries or university-hosted copies of original studies.
- **C — later scholarly synthesis:** useful for interpretation and follow-up, but not a replacement for the 1986–89 primary record.
- **D — popular/secondary lead:** not sufficient alone for a material claim; not used as support here.
