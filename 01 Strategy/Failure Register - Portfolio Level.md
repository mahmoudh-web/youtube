# Failure Register — Portfolio Level

Use this as a live register. Every failure point needs an observable signal, a preventive control, and a kill/pivot decision.

## Portfolio and strategy

1. Launching too many channels too early.
2. Choosing niches that are interesting but not clickable.
3. Making every channel look like generic AI content.
4. Running out of good, documented, safe, evergreen episodes.
5. Making channels too similar and causing audience overlap.
6. Building around cheap production instead of a real competitive advantage.

## Research and editorial

7. Research taking far longer than expected.
8. Publishing claims without a complete source pack or claim ledger.
9. Weak packaging: unclear titles, thumbnails, or central conflict.
10. Retention collapsing after the opening hook.
11. The founder becoming the approval bottleneck.

## Legal, policy, and commercial

12. Monetisation being delayed or rejected because content appears repetitive, reused, or mass-produced.
13. Scandal coverage creating legal problems through imprecise or unsupported claims.
14. Advertisers avoiding sensitive or sensational content.
15. International reach producing low-value audiences and disappointing revenue.

## Technology and economics

16. The AI PC failing to make production cheap after human time and operational costs are counted.
17. GPU capacity being mistaken for the main bottleneck when judgement and editing are the constraint.
18. Failed generations, lip-sync, audio, storage, maintenance, and workflow debugging being omitted from unit economics.

## Learning and governance

19. Views being mistaken for an audience.
20. No reliable feedback loop from titles, openings, retention, returning viewers, and monetisation.
21. Expanding because one Short went viral.
22. Committing six months without monthly kill-or-pivot reviews.

## Immediate controls

- Pilot no more than two channels initially.
- Require 100 candidate subjects and 20 fully researched subjects before launch.
- Require a source pack, claim ledger, visual plan, and packaging options before scripting.
- Measure cost per finished minute and total idea-to-upload time.
- Keep channel identities, subjects, voices, visuals, and upload patterns distinct.
- Treat AdSense as one layer, not the whole financial plan.
