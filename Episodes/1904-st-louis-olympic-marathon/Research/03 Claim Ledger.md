## Ledger conventions

“Confidence” measures how safely a claim can be stated with its source label attached. “Period report” means the source says it; it does not erase observer bias, later transcription or contradictory records. Every material claim needs a caveat where the sources do not agree.

## Not yet claimable

- A single definitive race-day temperature or humidity value.
- “Only one water station” without reconciling the six-mile tower and 12-mile well reports.
- Sullivan's intent as proven fact.
- An injection of one milligram of strychnine, rather than Lucas's reported oral one-sixtieth-grain doses.
- That Hicks was literally carried across the finish line, died later, or won because of the drug mixture.
- Rotten apples, a nap, a complete travel-to-St. Louis story, or the exact distance Lorz rode without a contemporary source.
- A precise starter fraction, “slowest-ever” superlative, exact race route, or exact dog detour until records are reconciled.
- Barefoot running or a claim that the South African runners were “Zulu” unless an appropriate source and terminology review supports it.
