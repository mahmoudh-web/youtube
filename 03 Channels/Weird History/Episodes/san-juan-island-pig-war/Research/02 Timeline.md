## Timeline interpretation

The sequence has two distinct escalations. First, a long-running treaty and jurisdiction conflict made a small livestock incident politically legible as a sovereignty test. Second, Harney's local military initiative converted a civil dispute into a military confrontation before Washington and London had authorised a final settlement. Scott's joint occupation prevented immediate war but preserved the unresolved title for twelve years. Arbitration finally ended the stand-off.
