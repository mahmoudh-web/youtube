# The Failure Report — Failure Points

- Technical explanations may be inaccurate or too dense.
- The channel may rely on difficult-to-license visuals.
- Disaster content may become exploitative or advertiser-unfriendly.
- Institutional responsibility may be oversimplified into a single villain.
- The premise may overlap with corporate, sports, or legal failures.

## Control

Use expert review for difficult claims, explain systems and incentives, avoid graphic or exploitative treatment, and define boundaries before building a topic database.
