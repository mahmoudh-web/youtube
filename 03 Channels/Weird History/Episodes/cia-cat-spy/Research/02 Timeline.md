## Timeline reading note

The evidence has three separate clocks: (1) the 1964–67 research and evaluation period, (2) the later 2001 public disclosure and press amplification, and (3) continuing FOIA and participant-memory disputes. The record supports “tested and abandoned as impractical” more securely than “one operational mission ended in a taxi.”
