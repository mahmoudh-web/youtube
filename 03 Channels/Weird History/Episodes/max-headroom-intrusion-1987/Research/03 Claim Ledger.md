## Classification controls

- **Fact:** direct event description or official log entry, with source limitations kept visible.
- **Allegation/attribution:** a person, station, newspaper or FOIA requester says or records something; preserve who is speaking.
- **Interpretation:** an inference from sequence, geography or content; label it and keep competing explanations.
- **Uncertainty:** an unresolved identity, motive, legal status, technical detail or negative finding.

No claim in this ledger is narration-approved. Independent validation must re-read the complete pack and check the underlying scans, recordings, FOIA response and technical records.
