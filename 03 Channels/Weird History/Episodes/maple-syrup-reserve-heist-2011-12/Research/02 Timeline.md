## Responsibility reconstruction

The record currently supports three distinct layers:

1. **Physical access and extraction:** the Supreme Court describes Caron’s operational control of the warehouse work and replacement of syrup with water or inferior syrup (S1 paras. 3–4).
2. **Receipt, resale and money trail:** the Court describes Vallières’s receipt and resale role, his control of $10m in sales income and his nearly $1m personal profit admission (S1 paras. 5–8).
3. **Legal responsibility:** Vallières’s 2016 jury convictions and the 2022 Supreme Court fine are judgments. Arrests, warrants, police theories and unreported participants are not equivalent findings.
