## Timeline conflicts to preserve

- The official Army history places the first shot shortly after 3:00 a.m.; Droke’s 1949 recollection says a meteorological balloon went up about 1:00 a.m. These may describe different balloons or different stages, but the records do not yet reconcile them.
- Contemporary Stimson reporting says 1,430 rounds; the official Army history says about 1,400. Do not silently harmonise them.
- Knox’s “false alarm,” the Army’s one-to-five assessment, and Stimson’s “as many as fifteen” are different official statements, not one settled count.
