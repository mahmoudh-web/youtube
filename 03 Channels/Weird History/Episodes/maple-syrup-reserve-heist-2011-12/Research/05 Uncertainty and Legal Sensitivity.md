## Established versus allegation language

### Cleared judicial findings for provisional use

- Vallières was convicted by a jury in 2016 of theft, fraud and trafficking in property obtained by crime (S1 para. 9).
- The Supreme Court described a year-long scheme involving warehouse extraction, substitution and resale (S1 paras. 3–8).
- The Supreme Court allowed the Crown appeal in 2022 and fixed the fine at $9,171,397.57 with a ten-year payment period and six-year default term (S1 para. 68; S2 lines 157–164). The three-year term belonged to the intermediate Quebec Court of Appeal order.

### Not cleared as guilt findings

- A person being arrested, sought, charged or named in a police release is not a conviction.
- The Federation’s 2012 descriptions of “illegal distribution” and an organized group are contemporaneous organizational/police assessments, not standalone proof against a named person.
- The media label “mastermind” should not replace the court’s specific role descriptions.
- Any claim about a particular co-accused’s conviction, plea or sentence requires the relevant Quebec judgment or a carefully sourced Canadian report checked against it.

## Legal and ethical flags

- **Defamation/fairness:** Several named participants may be living. Do not state or imply guilt beyond a documented conviction or guilty plea; preserve the date and court.
- **Privacy:** Avoid addresses, family details, business identifiers and personal financial details unless necessary to explain a verified court finding.
- **Jurisdiction:** Quebec judgments are in French and Canadian criminal-law terminology does not map cleanly onto casual English labels such as “fence,” “mastermind” or “cartel.”
- **Source balance:** Court records and police reports reconstruct the case from institutional perspectives. Seek human review of the producer and accused perspectives without treating either as automatically correct.
- **Image rights:** Warehouse, barrel, police and news images require separate provenance and licence checks. No image is cleared by this research note.
