# Corporate Case Files — Failure Points

- Allegations may be presented as facts.
- Civil misconduct may be incorrectly called criminal.
- Appeals, settlements, or company responses may be omitted.
- Living individuals may be exposed to avoidable legal risk.
- Cases may be too sensitive for advertisers.
- The niche may overlap with other scandal channels.

## Control

No episode proceeds without a source pack, claim ledger, legal-status check, response from the subject where relevant, and neutral educational framing.
