## Ledger conventions

“Confidence” measures how safely a claim can be stated with its source label attached. It is not a probability that a reconstruction is true. “Established record” means the document records the fact or finding. “Interpretation” means a source draws a conclusion from records. “Model output” means a scientific model produces a possible mechanism under assumptions. Every material claim carries an unresolved caveat where the source chain is incomplete or translations conflict.

## Not yet claimable

- “The avalanche caused the deaths” without attribution and a qualifier that this is the later prosecutor conclusion / modelled hypothesis.
- “The case is solved” or “the official cause is proven.”
- A single definitive slope angle, weather value, visibility figure or time of death.
- That the three traumatic deaths were caused by one identified object, person or event.
- That the victims were carried or dragged from the tent after being injured; the tracks and paper model do not establish the full movement sequence.
- That beta contamination proves a Mayak, missile or nuclear-test connection.
- That “violent death” means murder.
- That the Mansi, Soviet military, investigators, researchers or any living person caused or concealed a crime without primary evidence and legal review.
