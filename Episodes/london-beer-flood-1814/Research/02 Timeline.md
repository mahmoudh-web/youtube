## Chronology guardrails

- The period report's broad window is **about 5:30–6:00 p.m.** The 4:30 figure in later retellings may describe an earlier hoop observation, not the rupture itself; preserve the distinction.
- The **3,555 barrels** belongs to the failed vat. The reported **7,664 / 8,000–9,000 barrels** belongs to combined losses and may include the neighbouring vessel and casks.
- “The mother and daughter died” is not safe without attribution: the inquest text identifies Hannah Banfield as dead and says her mother was taken to hospital; another period report may describe the mother differently.
- The contemporary inquest establishes **eight** deaths. A later ninth-death tradition exists but is not part of that verdict.
