## Mechanism sequence under review

The best-supported sequence is: magmatic CO₂ entered the lake system over time → dense, chemically stratified deep water stored the gas → an initiating disturbance or instability caused rapid exsolution and upward movement → a dense CO₂-rich cloud flowed down the drainage valleys. The first, second and fourth links are strongly supported by the located record; the third remains unresolved. The Italian mission’s phreatic-explosion scenario is a competing explanation for that third link and possibly for the release dynamics more broadly.

## Date and wording controls

- Use **21 August 1986** throughout. A university outreach page incorrectly gives 26 August; it is not used.
- Use “around 22:00” only when clearly attributed to the witness-reconstruction paper.
- Use “at least 1,700,” “1,746” or “1,700–2,000” with the named source; do not silently reconcile the counts.
- Treat “explosion,” “eruption” and “detonation” as source language requiring context. The evidence does not describe a conventional fireball or bomb blast.
