# Validation Gates

These gates protect the project from spending heavily before the idea proves itself.

## Gate 1 — Idea depth

- 100 credible candidate subjects
- 20 fully researched candidates
- At least 3 repeatable content pillars
- No dependence on rumours, weak summaries, or unresolved material unless current affairs is explicitly the format

## Gate 2 — Episode viability

- Complete source pack
- Claim ledger
- Legal/status check
- Visual plan and copyright review
- Five title and thumbnail concepts
- A clear unanswered question or conflict

## Gate 3 — Audience evidence

- Multiple subjects attract viewers
- Returning viewers increase
- Long-form performance is not being confused with Shorts reach
- Retention shows the format can sustain a full episode

## Gate 4 — Economics

- Cost per finished minute recorded
- GPU hours, failed generations, human editing, storage, electricity, and maintenance included
- Revenue assumptions include non-AdSense options
- Ownership of expensive hardware is justified by measured workload

## Gate 5 — Scale or stop

Scale only after a meaningful batch shows repeatable audience interest and a workable production loop. Otherwise kill, pivot, or archive the hypothesis with a written reason.
