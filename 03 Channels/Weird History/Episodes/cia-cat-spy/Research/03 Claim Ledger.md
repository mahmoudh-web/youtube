## Ledger conventions

Each row contains one material claim. “Confidence” describes the safest wording currently supportable, not certainty that the surviving record is complete. CIA statements are attributed to the Agency. Redacted documents and retrospective testimony are never silently upgraded into an unqualified fact. “Acoustic Kitty” is retained as the public/later label pending verification of the redacted title and codename chain.

## Wording gate

Current wording that may advance only after human verification:

> The CIA really did test a cat fitted with listening equipment. Its surviving 1967 memo says cats could be trained to move short distances, but the technique was not practical for specialized intelligence work; the CIA now says the program ended in 1967 without operational use. The famous taxi death, exact cost and precise device layout belong to a contested afterlife of testimony and press retelling, not to a fully open mission record.

This is not approved narration. It is the current claim-ledger wording gate and remains subject to human source checking.
