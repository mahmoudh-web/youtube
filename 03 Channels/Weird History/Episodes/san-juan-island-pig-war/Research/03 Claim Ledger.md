## Claims not cleared for narration

- “The British threatened to arrest Cutlar and evict every American” — the threat appears in later summaries, but the contemporaneous accounts conflict. Use only with explicit attribution and primary-source verification.
- “Harney invaded for strategic reasons” — possible and supported as an NPS interpretation, but not proven by the evidence collected here. Keep as an open motive question.
- Exact troop and gun counts — NPS and later summaries vary; use ranges or verify musters and orders.
- “The pig was the only casualty” — safe only as a qualified label for the U.S.-British military standoff, never as a claim that nobody on or around the island suffered harm.
- Any claim that Coast Salish peoples had no role in the 1859–72 story — contradicted by the park's own context and not acceptable.
