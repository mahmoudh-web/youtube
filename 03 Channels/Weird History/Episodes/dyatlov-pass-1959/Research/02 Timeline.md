## Chronology principles

This is an evidence timeline, not a reconstructed script. Dates in the first section come mainly from the 1959 case-file transcription. Later dates identify when an investigation or paper made a claim, not when a newly discovered fact necessarily occurred.

## Sequence that remains unobserved

No located source contains a surviving eyewitness account of the trigger, the group’s first movements, the exact time of death, or the transport of injured people to the forest/ravine. The 2020 reconstruction and the 2021 model fill those gaps with inference; the ledger must label those passages accordingly.
