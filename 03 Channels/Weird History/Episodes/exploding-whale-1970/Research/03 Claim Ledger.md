## Claims not cleared for narration

- “The governor/ODOT headquarters ordered the explosion.” No such authorization has been located.
- “The Navy told them to use exactly 1,000 pounds.” The consultation is reported, but the advice and quantity decision are not documented in a located primary record.
- “Thornton acted negligently,” “ignored a warning,” or “was fired/promoted because of the blast.” These are legal or causal conclusions not established by the current pack.
- “No one was harmed” or “the only casualty was a whale.” The accounts report no human injury but document a damaged car, near-miss debris, exposure and later cleanup; the “only casualty” line is too broad.
- “This was the first viral video,” “most watched news report ever,” or an exact lifetime view count. Treat these as attributed media-history claims, not cleared facts.
