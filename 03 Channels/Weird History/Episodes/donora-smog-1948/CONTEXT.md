## Purpose

This folder is the source-led research area for the provisional episode “Donora’s Death Fog: The Smog That Put Air Pollution on Trial,” about the October 1948 air-pollution episode in Donora and Webster, Pennsylvania. It is a research pack, not a script or production brief.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve a traceable source index, timeline, claim ledger, counterevidence record, uncertainty and human-verification note, and provisional conclusion.
- Reconstruct the episode through the 1949 U.S. Public Health Service investigation, Pennsylvania archival records, later follow-up studies, and policy records.
- Distinguish the contemporaneous death count from later summaries and state exactly what population, dates, and definitions each count covers.
- Separate established fact, source-attributed allegation, interpretation, and uncertainty. Do not assign sole chemical or legal responsibility where the record does not support it.
- Stop for human source verification before narration, packaging, visual generation, editing, QC, or publication.

## Key Decisions

- Use `donora-smog-1948` as the episode folder slug under the authoritative Weird History channel folder.
- Use the 1949 Public Health Service count of 20 deaths and 5,910 affected people (42.7% of the Donora area population) as the primary wording gate, while recording 19-in-24-hours, 21, and 22 as scope or later-summary discrepancies requiring review.
- Treat the event as a combined pollution-and-weather episode. The primary investigation found multiple contributors and did not establish one responsible substance; avoid presenting hydrogen fluoride, sulfur dioxide, the zinc works, or American Steel and Wire as a sole proven cause.
- Treat the 1949 Pennsylvania Division of Air Pollution Control, the 1950 federal technical conference, and later federal legislation as a qualified policy sequence in which Donora contributed evidence and public pressure, not as a simple one-event legal chain.
- Keep the phrase “put air pollution on trial” as packaging language only. The 1951 civil litigation was reportedly settled without an adjudication of blame in the located secondary account.

## Constraints and Invariants

- Access date for this initial pack: 2026-09-16.
- The PHS report is a preliminary report and the EPA-hosted text is a later educational reproduction of its appendix; the original scan must be checked before quotation or page-specific claims.
- No air-quality measurements were taken during the acute episode itself. Later sampling and the 1949 investigation support source and mechanism analysis but are not a time-stamped measurement series from 27–31 October.
- “Affected,” “ill,” “severely affected,” “death during or shortly after,” and “long-term mortality” are different categories. Never substitute one for another.
- The pack contains no script, title/thumbnail package, visual brief, downloaded archival asset, rights clearance, edit, or publication recommendation.
