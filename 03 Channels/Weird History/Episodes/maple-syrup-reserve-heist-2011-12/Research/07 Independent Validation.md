## Source quality and coverage

### Strengths

- **Strong primary anchor:** the official SCC decision, case docket, case brief, and official filed factums support the final legal disposition, the central roles, the offence period, the $10m admission, and the principal figures.
- **Official investigative record:** the archived SQ activity report directly supports Project LUISANCE, searches, accused count and the 67% figure, with the important limitation that it is an institutional activity summary.
- **Useful contemporaneous victim record:** the Federation releases preserve the inventory discovery, security description, reserve purpose and changing early estimates. They are interested organizational communications and correctly remain attributed.
- **Independent reporting cross-check:** RCI and Canadian Press reporting corroborate the investigation window, interviews, geography, arrests and public estimates while preserving their time-specific status.
- **Regulatory context:** the Régie report supports the reserve’s stabilizing role and Federation/collective-marketing context, but it is not evidence of individual criminal responsibility.

### Coverage limits

- The original 2017 QCCS and 2020 QCCA reasons were not readable through the cited CanLII URLs in this environment. The official SCC docket confirms their dates, amounts and default terms, but it does not replace a human reading of the French reasons.
- The exact 9,571-barrel / 5,935,250-lb / $17,805,750 accounting is reproduced in the official party factums; the accessible SCC reasons confirm the over-$18m scale but are concise. This exact accounting should remain a court-record figure pending the lower-court check.
- The pack does not establish final dispositions for every accused, the complete trial exhibit trail, the inventory worksheet, access logs, surveillance, insurance file, or the underlying recovery/seizure accounting.
- “67% recovered,” “about two-thirds traced,” “about 60%,” and the initial “over 10m lb” report are not interchangeable measures. The pack correctly flags this, but any script must retain source/date/verb distinctions.
- A 2012 SCC case brief says 16 people were arrested, while the later SQ report says 23 people were accused and RCI says 28 arrested or sought. These are different dated denominators, not a contradiction to be collapsed into one total.

## Unsupported, overstated, or contradictory items

1. C18’s exact “Great Canadian Maple Syrup Heist” label is unsupported by the listed citations. Remove it or source it separately.
2. C02–C03 should not be described as wholly neutral “judicial findings” until the French trial reasons are read; the accessible exact figures occur in party factums reproducing the trial record.
3. Any use of “mastermind,” “ringleader,” “insider,” “cartel,” “mafia,” “black market,” or “organized crime” needs either a direct source attribution or narrower wording. “Black market” appears in the SCC background; it should not be converted into a broad factual claim about the whole industry.
4. The Global News sentencing report is sufficient to establish that three men were reported convicted and sentenced, but not to supply the precise legal count or final status of every other accused. Use the French judgments or outcome-specific records for person-level claims.
5. “Break-in” or forced entry is not established by the cited SCC passages. The Federation used “burglarized” in an initial release, while the Court’s account emphasizes sustained access, extraction and substitution. The pack’s narrower wording is the safer one.
