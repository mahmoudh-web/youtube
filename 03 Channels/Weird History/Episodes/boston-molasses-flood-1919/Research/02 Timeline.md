## Chronology controls

- The date of a death certificate is not automatically the date of the flood. The official index itself includes later dates for some victims.
- “Explosion” is period language. The central causal question is whether a bomb/high explosive caused the tank’s failure or whether the tank failed structurally, potentially with internal gas/temperature effects.
- The decisive 1925 document is an auditor’s report. The civil case’s settlement means “court found” must be qualified as “the court-appointed auditor found.”
