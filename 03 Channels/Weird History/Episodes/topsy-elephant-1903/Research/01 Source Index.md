## Research question

What is documented about Topsy’s execution and filming, and how did the documented Luna Park/utility/film-company chain become the stronger but unproven story that Thomas Edison personally staged it to discredit alternating current?

## Working answer and scope

The located record supports a narrower account than the familiar internet version. Luna Park officials decided Topsy had to be killed; the ASPCA/SPCA objected to the planned hanging and was involved in the alternative method according to institutional retrospectives; and the local Edison Electric Illuminating Company of Brooklyn supplied technical assistance. The Edison Manufacturing Company filmed the electrocution and registered the film under Thomas A. Edison’s name. The Edison Papers’ dedicated review says Edison is absent from contemporaneous reports and extant correspondence and finds no evidence he directed either the execution or filming. The film’s survival and sensational title plausibly helped fuse a local utility, a film company and the inventor into one later “technology argument,” but that motive is an interpretation, not a proved 1903 intention.

## Source-quality key

- **A — primary/contemporary:** newspaper, period film/catalogue, copyright record or archive item.
- **B — authoritative institutional:** Library of Congress, Edison Papers/Rutgers, library archive or museum catalogue.
- **C — peer-reviewed scholarship:** historical or film scholarship with cited primary sources.
- **D — modern secondary/lead:** useful for discovery or reception history; not sufficient alone for a material narration claim.
