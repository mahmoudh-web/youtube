## Ledger conventions

“Confidence” measures how safely a claim can be stated with its source label attached. “Established report” means the period report says it, not that the claim is free from witness or transmission bias. Open caveats must travel with the claim.

## Not yet claimable

- A single definitive total such as “1.5 million litres” without explaining whether it counts only the failed vat, all vessels, or a later conversion.
- A modern engineering diagnosis stated as if it were recorded in 1814; “hydrostatic pressure” should be labelled as interpretation unless an engineer validates it against the testimony.
- “The brewery was negligent” or “the coroner cleared negligence” without legal-history review of the inquest standard and the original proceedings.
- A ninth death from alcohol poisoning, mass drunkenness, a hospital riot, or a body-viewing-floor collapse as established facts.
- The nationality, religion, occupation, or precise home address of an individual victim beyond what the report names.
