# Weird History

**Status:** Unvalidated hypothesis

## Provisional promise

Documented absurd events from around the world.

## Failure points

- Folklore and repeated internet claims may be mistaken for documented history.
- Strange stories may be interesting but not visually or narratively strong.
- Global scope increases source and translation complexity.
- The channel may drift into generic trivia.
