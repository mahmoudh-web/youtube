## Mechanism comparison

| Question | CO₂ accumulation / limnological interpretation | Phreatic-explosion interpretation | What the record permits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where did the lethal gas come from? | Magmatic CO₂ entered groundwater/lake water and accumulated at depth. | A volcanic process beneath the lake released gas and water. | Magmatic origin is strongly supported; the Italian mission’s broader phreatic explanation remains a documented competing interpretation. |
| Where was the gas immediately before release? | Dissolved in deep, dense, stratified lake water. | Released from a subsurface volcanic source or through a phreatic event. | USGS/Science water chemistry and isotopes support substantial pre-storage in the lake; this does not alone identify the trigger. |
| What caused the sudden release? | An external disturbance or internal buoyancy/overturn instability; no single trigger established. | A phreatic explosion beneath the lake bed. | Both are published interpretations. The USGS report explicitly calls the mechanism undetermined. |
| What evidence would support direct volcanism? | — | Hot water, volcanic gases, disturbed sediment, acid gases, eruption products or other direct activity. | US investigators reported clear/deep water, low temperatures, no major sediment disturbance and lack of sulfur/acid-gas signatures as evidence against significant direct volcanic activity. |
| Why was there a water surge? | Rapid gas exsolution and upward expansion can move water. | Explosive subsurface activity can drive a wave/fountain. | A surge is observed/reported, but it does not discriminate by itself. |

## Candidate triggers and controls

| Candidate | Evidence for | Evidence against / limitation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subaqueous landslide or rockfall | Geologically plausible; landslides are linked to the 1984 Monoun event. | No Nyos-specific proof is established in the core USGS summary; witness evidence and sediment observations require close reading. | Plausible candidate, not fact. |
| Earthquake | Could disturb a stratified lake and initiate overturn. | No confirmed Nyos-specific seismic event is located in this pack; absence of felt shaking is not a complete negative. | Possible but unverified. |
| Heavy rain, storm or wind-driven mixing | Storms occurred before the event; wind/turbulence can disturb lake layers. | Timing and energy threshold are not demonstrated; the USGS/Kling work says no single common process is required by the available data. | Possible mechanism class, not established trigger. |
| Temperature differential / seasonal weakening | August timing and changing stratification were discussed in the scientific literature; seasonal instability is physically plausible. | Observations do not prove a specific temperature event caused the release. | Later limnological hypothesis. |
| Internal buoyancy-reversal instability | Cotel’s model gives a physically plausible internal trigger as gas loading increases. | It is a later model; the historical event was not instrumented well enough to confirm its criteria. | Model-based interpretation. |
| Direct phreatic eruption | Italian mission reported field observations it interpreted as consistent with a phreatic explosion. | US investigators found no significant direct volcanic activity indicators; the competing papers interpret the same broad evidence differently. | Published competing interpretation. |
| Fermentation of organic material | Organic matter can produce gas in some settings. | Wagner et al. report C14 evidence eliminating fermentation as the explanation for the relevant carbon source; CO₂ chemistry and isotopes point elsewhere. | Not supported as the main source in this pack. |
| Dam failure or conventional flood | Lake had a weak natural dam and a water surge was observed. | The dam-failure study treats collapse as an additional future hazard, not the cause of the 1986 valley deaths; gas/asphyxia evidence is independent. | Separate hazard; do not merge. |

## Evidence against over-simple retellings

### “It was a volcano”

Lake Nyos sits in a volcanic setting and the carbon was magmatic, but those facts do not prove a direct eruption occurred on 21 August 1986. The USGS/Science record explicitly separates the deep source of CO₂ from the absence of significant direct volcanic activity during the disaster. The Italian mission’s phreatic explanation must be attributed as a competing interpretation.

### “It was just a gas bubble”

The gas was not documented as a single free-floating bubble under the lake. The strongest US record describes CO₂ dissolved in deep bottom water and stabilized by lake stratification. Explain dissolved gas, pressure and density without inventing a physical object.

### “The water wave proves a tsunami/flood”

A water wave or surge is part of the field and witness record, but the medical and geographic evidence points to a CO₂-rich cloud as the principal lethal agent. The dam-failure hazard was separately assessed by USGS.

### “Nobody had symptoms”

Rapid unconsciousness and death are reported, but survivors were medically evaluated and some had lesions. The BMJ study revised the interpretation of many lesions while noting that other toxic factors could not be entirely excluded. Use bounded medical wording.

### “The trigger is solved by the word limnic”

“Limnic eruption” labels a class of sudden gas releases from lakes; it does not itself identify what initiated this one. Keep the release class, gas source, and trigger as separate analytical layers.

## Counterevidence checklist for human review

- Read the USGS report pages covering field observations, sediment, chemistry, isotopes, temperature and trigger discussion rather than relying on the executive summary alone.
- Obtain the complete Italian paper and record which observations are direct measurements versus interpretation.
- Compare the witness reconstruction with the USGS introduction and record discrepancies in time, plume, wave and sound descriptions.
- Verify the medical study’s sample sizes, terminology and conclusion about skin lesions before using any health claim.
- Check the Cameroon ministry’s 2016 wording in the original PDF and preserve it as an official retrospective, not an independent re-analysis.
- Keep the weak-dam flood hazard and artificial degassing history in separate sections from the 1986 trigger.
