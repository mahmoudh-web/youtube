## Timeline interpretation

The crisis moved through three overlapping tracks: competing legal/geographic claims, coercive local enforcement backed by militia, and a federal statehood decision. The “war” label captures the rhetoric and mobilisation but obscures how little sustained combat the record establishes. The final settlement is best described as Ohio retaining the disputed strip, Michigan accepting a revised boundary and entering the Union, and the revised boundary including most of the present Upper Peninsula beyond the eastern section already included in Michigan’s 1835 proposal (S19). Whether that is a “trade” or “compensation” is an interpretation, not the statutory wording.
