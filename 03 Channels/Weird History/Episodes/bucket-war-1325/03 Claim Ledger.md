## Not yet claimable

- “Bologna declared war because Modena stole its only bucket.”
- “The surviving bucket is proven to be the exact object taken on 15 November 1325.”
- “The Modenese entered Bologna and stole the bucket during the battle” without attributing it to a later tradition and resolving the conflicting chronicle/poem chronology.
- Any exact casualty total or army size without the critical Griffoni passage and source criticism.
- “Guelph Bologna fought Ghibelline Modena for purely ideological reasons.”
- “Tassoni invented the bucket story from nothing.” Local traditions may have predated the poem; the evidence here supports dissemination and reshaping, not a claim about absolute invention.
