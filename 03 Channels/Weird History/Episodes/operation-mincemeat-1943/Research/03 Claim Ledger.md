## Not yet claimable

- “The corpse won the war,” “saved 30,000 lives,” or any other unreferenced numerical effect.
- “Hitler moved troops because of Mincemeat alone.”
- “The Spanish government knowingly collaborated” without naming the official, document and degree of access established by the records.
- “The body was definitely Glyndwr Michael” without qualifying the released-record and scholarly basis and noting the *Dasher* dispute.
- “The Spanish doctors were fooled by a complete autopsy” or any claim about the precise post-mortem findings without the original Spanish record.
- “Ian Fleming invented Mincemeat,” “Churchill personally designed it,” or any other single-author claim not supported by the operation files.
- “The papers were read exactly as planted.” The German report describes handling and apparent resealing; the evidentiary chain is more complicated.
