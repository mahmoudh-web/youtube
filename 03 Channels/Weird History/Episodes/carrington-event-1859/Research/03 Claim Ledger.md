## Ledger conventions

Confidence describes how safely a claim can be stated with its source and qualifier attached. “Observed” means recorded in a contemporary document or preserved instrument record. “Inferred” means a later interpretation, reconstruction or model. Every open caveat travels with the claim.

## Not yet claimable

- “The internet went down” or “the internet would definitely collapse.”
- “Every telegraph office caught fire,” “the whole telegraph network failed,” or a precise global outage percentage.
- A precise CME speed, mass, flare class or Dst value unless the cited modern reconstruction and its assumptions are named and checked.
- A universal repair time, global death toll, global blackout or exact economic cost.
- “Carrington caused X” where the source only shows temporal coincidence or a later model inference.
- A verbatim telegraph dialogue until the original newspaper scan and copy chain have been checked.
