## Confidence key

- **High:** directly supported by a primary or official record located in this pack.
- **Medium:** supported by a named scholarly or institutional synthesis that cites an underlying record, but the original has not yet been inspected.
- **Low:** plausible or widely repeated but missing direct proof, geographically mixed, or dependent on an unresolved causal inference.
