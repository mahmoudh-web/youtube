## Ledger conventions

Confidence describes how safely a claim can be stated with the source label attached. It does not mean that the historical event has a known cause. “Open caveat” travels with the claim into any later editorial work.

## Not yet claimable

- “Frau Troffea” as a verified named person, address, marriage or motive.
- A precise total such as 400 dancers, 15 deaths per day, or hundreds dead.
- Any exact death count, cause of death, or claim that people literally danced until cardiac arrest.
- Ergotism, mass hysteria, conversion disorder, demonic possession or a particular neurological disease as the solved cause.
- A complete “city hired musicians, built stages, and made people dance” sequence without a source label and the Clementz/Specklin caveat.
- “The pilgrimage cured the outbreak” rather than “the episode declined after the city sent some affected people to Saverne.”
