# Software Automation

**Status:** Unvalidated hypothesis

## Provisional promise

Software, automation, and useful systems explained through practical mechanisms and real-world applications.

## Failure points

- Tools and APIs change quickly, weakening evergreen value.
- Tutorials may be country-, platform-, or version-specific.
- Demonstrations may become obsolete before publication.
- The channel may require more hands-on testing than the other ideas.
