## Purpose

This folder is the source-led research area for the provisional episode “The BBC Spaghetti Tree: When Panorama Harvested Pasta,” about the BBC’s 1 April 1957 *Panorama* hoax report set in Ticino, Switzerland. It is a research pack, not a script or production brief.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve a traceable source index, chronology, claim ledger, counterevidence record, uncertainty note and provisional conclusion.
- Separate the fabricated harvest report from established facts about *Panorama*, the BBC production team, Ticino, the broadcast record and documented audience responses.
- Record exact page/line/audio/video locators, access dates, confidence and unresolved caveats.
- Stop for independent human validation before narration, packaging, visual generation, editing, QC or publication.

## Key Decisions

- Use `bbc-spaghetti-tree-1957` as the episode folder slug under the authoritative Weird History channel folder.
- Treat the “spaghetti harvest,” “spaghetti weevil” and tree-grown pasta as invented claims inside a broadcast hoax, never as historical agriculture.
- Prefer the BBC Programme Index, surviving BBC Archive footage/audio, the 1957 newspaper reproduction, and firsthand or near-firsthand production accounts. Use later summaries only to triangulate or identify retrieval leads.
- Keep audience size, number of callers, degree of belief, exact filming location and the full crew credit qualified until primary BBC records are checked.

## Constraints and Invariants

- Access date for this pack is 2026-09-16.
- “Millions watched” is an audience estimate or retrospective summary; it is not evidence that millions believed the report.
- Calls, letters and family arguments show reception among documented respondents, not a representative survey of Britain.
- No current source establishes a complete call log, exact number of letters, or a population-level belief rate.
- Visual and audio sources are archival leads only. Rights, licence scope, and whether platform use is permitted require a separate human decision.
