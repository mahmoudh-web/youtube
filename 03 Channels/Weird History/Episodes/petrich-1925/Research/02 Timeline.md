## Date-control notes

- The dog story is often dated 18 October, while the League catalog and official summaries use 19 or 22 October. Preserve the date range until the original telegrams and Commission report are compared.
- The 22 October date is securely tied to Bulgaria’s League appeal in the official League case summary; it is not necessarily the date of the first frontier shot.
- Greek withdrawal was ordered and observed through the League mechanism by 28–29 October; this is separate from the later finding of responsibility and compensation.
- “Ten-day war” is a label built from selected endpoints, not a substitute for the Council’s event sequence.
