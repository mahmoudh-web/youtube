## Reading note

“Established” means supported by a primary/official record or contemporaneous medical paper. “Attributed” means a named source reports the event or interpretation. “Association” means timing or location overlap without causal proof. “Uncertainty” marks a discrepancy or missing record.

| Date | Event | Classification | Support and caveat |

| 1946 | A Fort Detrick study exposed healthy volunteers to large amounts of *Serratia marcescens* 8 UK; later legal testimony treated the short-lived symptoms as evidence of low risk. | Attributed | S2, pp. 189–191; S4, pp. 118–119. This was a controlled volunteer study, not evidence that an urban release was safe for a heterogeneous civilian population. |
| 1948–49 | U.S. officials developed vulnerability-testing plans in response to concern about covert biological attack. | Established programme context | S4, Army history section and pp. 37–38; exact committee/approval chain needs the underlying files. |

The later “Operation Sea-Spray” label should not obscure the report's formal title. |

| 22 Jan 1951 | McNulty's Special Report 142 was issued by the Chemical Corps Biological Laboratories, Camp Detrick. | Established catalogue fact | S1 and S14. The original document's classification markings and release history require direct inspection. |
| 1951 | Wheat, Zuckerman and Rantz published the Stanford case series in *Archives of Internal Medicine*. | Established | S5–S6. The abstract calls the infections rare and notes one fatal endocarditis case. |
| 1952 | The Army's later review discussed whether the San Francisco cases were coincidental and recommended hospital follow-up for future populated-area tests. | Attributed official response | S4 pp. 144–146 / OCR lines 11517–11544. The OCR misdates the Wheat paper in places; use the journal record for publication date. |

| 2011 | A peer-reviewed review summarised the Stanford cluster, missing strain archive and later CDC comparison. | Scholarly synthesis | S9. Verify against S8 and the original medical/legal records. |

## Timeline stop rule

Do not collapse the 20–27 September six-trial report into a single release date or the broader programme into “one experiment.” Do not convert the September Stanford association into a settled death claim. The original technical report, full medical paper, CDC study and district record remain required checks.
