## Research Question and Promise

**Question:** Which 23rd Headquarters Special Troops deceptions demonstrably altered German expectations, and what do official and enemy records prove rather than merely suggest?

**Promise:** Test the celebrated “Ghost Army” story operation by operation, retaining both successful effects and records that show incomplete or mixed deception.

## Source-Quality Key

- **A — primary/near-primary:** wartime operation reports, official unit history, captured records, or firsthand veteran material.
- **B — official secondary:** U.S. Army educational or institutional synthesis that identifies primary records.
- **C — reputable secondary:** scholarly analysis used for interpretation and counterevidence.
- **D — summary/lead:** useful for locating evidence or documenting later estimates; not sufficient alone for a material claim.

## Source Gaps

This pack does not yet have direct scans of the NARA operation reports, the German T78 frames, the full Eldridge article, or the original LOC audiovisual interviews. Those are human-verification requirements, not invitations to fill gaps with repeated web summaries.
