## Purpose

This folder is the source-led research area for the provisional episode “The Elephant Death Filmed as a Technology Argument.” It investigates Topsy’s 4 January 1903 killing at Luna Park, Coney Island, the Edison Manufacturing Company film, and the later attribution of the event to Thomas Edison’s AC/DC rivalry. It is a research pack, not a script or production brief.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve a traceable source index, timeline, claim ledger, counterevidence record, uncertainty/legal-sensitivity note and provisional conclusion.
- Separate contemporaneous newspapers and copyright/film records from institutional retrospectives, scholarship and modern retellings.
- Distinguish documented responsibility for the execution, technical assistance and filming from the unproven claim that Thomas Edison personally ordered the event as an anti-AC demonstration.
- Record exact film timestamps only against the identified transfer; do not treat a restoration’s runtime as the original release length.
- Stop for human verification before narration, packaging, visual generation, editing, QC or publication.

## Key Decisions

- Use `topsy-elephant-1903` as the episode folder slug under the authoritative Weird History channel folder.
- Use “Topsy was killed at Luna Park and filmed by Edison Manufacturing” as the supported core; use “Edison personally staged it to discredit AC” only as a claim to investigate and reject or qualify.
- Treat the local electric utility’s Edison name and the Edison Manufacturing film credit as separate corporate links from Thomas Edison’s personal involvement.
- Treat the 1903 execution as a real death spectacle and the later AC/DC framing as a memory-making problem, not as settled motive.

## Constraints and Invariants

- Access date for this pack is 2026-09-14.
- The source record conflicts on voltage (6,000/6,600), poison amount/unit (grams versus grains), crowd size and the number of earlier deaths. Preserve attribution and do not harmonise these figures without checking scans.
- The Internet Archive derivative inspected here runs 95.26 seconds and includes modern title/credit cards; the period catalogue describes 70 feet, commonly timed at about 74 seconds. Timestamp notes must name the transfer.
- “No evidence in Edison’s extant correspondence” is a bounded archival finding, not proof that no undocumented conversation occurred.
- Avoid graphic animal-death imagery and sensational metadata. Human editorial review must decide whether any footage is necessary, whether it can be shown briefly with context, and whether platform, rights and audience safeguards are sufficient.
