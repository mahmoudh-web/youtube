## Ledger key

- **Fact:** supported by an inspected source as worded.
- **Official statement:** a source records what an official or institution said; it is not automatically true.
- **Allegation:** a claim made by a contemporary actor without sufficient corroboration.
- **Interpretation:** a later explanation or editorial framing.
- **Uncertainty:** the located record does not settle the point.
