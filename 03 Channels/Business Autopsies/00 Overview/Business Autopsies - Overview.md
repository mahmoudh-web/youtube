# Business Autopsies

**Status:** Unvalidated hypothesis

## Provisional promise

Company failures, unusual business models, and economic mysteries.

## Failure points

- Business analysis may become hindsight bias or unsupported speculation.
- Financial records may take longer to interpret than expected.
- Defamation risk can arise around failed companies and living executives.
- The audience may overlap with Corporate Case Files.
