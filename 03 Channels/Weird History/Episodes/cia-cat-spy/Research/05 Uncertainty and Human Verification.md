## Language and translation checks

- Keep “Acoustic Kitty” and “Acoustikitty” spelling as source-specific; do not silently normalize the name until the codename’s official status is established.
- Distinguish “microphone,” “transmitter,” “antenna,” “control,” “guidance” and “recording”; the sources do not prove that every component or capability coexisted in one final prototype.
- Avoid euphemistic or graphic surgical language. Use “implanted listening equipment” in factual prose and attribute anatomical detail.
- Distinguish “field test,” “demonstration,” “simulated operation” and “operational mission.” They are not interchangeable.
- Do not translate CIA’s “harmed/unharmed” statement into a broader claim that the project was ethically acceptable by contemporary or present standards.
