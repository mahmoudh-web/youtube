## Claim-ledger rules

- Every material sentence in a later script must map to one or more IDs above or to a newly verified row.
- A police release or news report may establish that an arrest, search or public statement occurred; it does not establish guilt.
- A court’s statement of the case background is stronger than a media paraphrase, but a human must still inspect the underlying French judgment where role precision matters.
- No claim is narration-ready until the unresolved caveat column has been cleared or deliberately carried into the wording.
