## Claims held back

- “The objects flew over the White House and Capitol” — unsupported by an exact target plot in the accessible source set; do not use. “Washington area” is the safe geographic wording pending original plots.
- “The objects reached 7,000 mph” — a later advocacy/retrospective calculation, not established by the strongest case-file wording; do not use.
- “The jets saw and chased an alien craft” — unsupported; the case file records F-94 dispatch and reported lights/negative intercept results but missing official interceptor reports.
- “The Air Force covered up the truth” — unsupported; secrecy, incomplete records and public-relations concerns do not prove a specific conspiracy.
- “Truman personally demanded an explanation” — unsupported by a direct presidential record in the reviewed sources; CIA history’s White House-concern wording is not proof of personal action.
- “Secret Soviet/American technology was responsible” — unsupported; retain only as historical Cold War context and an unproven hypothesis.
- “The sightings were extraterrestrial” — unsupported; “unidentified” is not evidence of extraterrestrial origin.
- “The CAA proved the temperature inversion explanation” — too broad; Report 180 tabulates July records but formally concludes on August tracked observations.
