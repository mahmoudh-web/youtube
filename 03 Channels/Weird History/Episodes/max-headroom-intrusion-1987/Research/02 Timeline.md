## Sequence interpretation

The sequence suggests an adaptive or at least repeatable operation: the first interruption was brief and WGN recovered by changing a link frequency; the second was longer and WTTW’s staff could not immediately restore the programme. That is an interpretation from the contrasting reports, not proof that the same person operated both events or that the perpetrator deliberately “switched” stations.
