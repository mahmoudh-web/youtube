## Purpose

This folder is the source-led research area for the provisional episode “The Max Headroom Intrusion: Who Hijacked Chicago Television? (1987).” It is an evidence pack, not a script or production brief.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve a traceable source index, timeline, claim ledger, counterevidence record, uncertainty/legal-sensitivity note and provisional conclusion.
- Separate established fact, allegation or attribution, interpretation and uncertainty.
- Reconstruct the different outcomes of the WGN and WTTW interruptions without treating them as one identical event.
- Keep the technical route, motive and identity questions open where the public record does not resolve them.
- Stop for independent human validation before narration, packaging, visual generation, editing, QC or publication.

## Key Decisions

- Use `max-headroom-intrusion-1987` as the episode slug under the authoritative Weird History channel folder.
- Treat “hijack” or “intrusion” as the description used by broadcasters and contemporary reporting; the exact RF path and equipment remain unproven in the located public record.
- Treat WTTW’s 2017 engineering recollection as an attributed retrospective, not as a contemporaneous FCC technical finding.
- Treat FBI FOIA-log entries and the FCC’s 2022 “no responsive documents” response as records about later records requests. They do not establish the contents of a 1987 FBI/FCC case file.
- Exclude uncharged suspect names and internet theories from the working fact record.

## Constraints and Invariants

- Access date for this pack is 2026-09-16; source URLs and page locators must be rechecked by an independent validator.
- The two interruptions have different durations and recoveries: WGN returned after roughly half a minute when its engineer changed the link frequency; WTTW’s intrusion ran for roughly 90 seconds and ended on its own according to the station’s retrospective.
- “Sophisticated technical knowledge” is an engineer’s assessment. It is not proof of an insider, a ham-radio operator, a particular transmitter, or any named suspect.
- Brief nudity and sexualised parody are part of the historical broadcast record. Do not reproduce more detail than is necessary for verification, and defer treatment to human editorial review.
- The archive copies and public video pages are research leads, not cleared reuse licences.
