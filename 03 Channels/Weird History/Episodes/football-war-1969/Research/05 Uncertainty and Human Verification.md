## Confidence policy

- **High:** primary record supports the bounded wording, but it may still be incomplete or partisan.
- **Medium:** multiple sources converge, but causal weight, attribution or exact measurement remains open.
- **Low:** a claim appears in a secondary/tertiary source or conflicts materially with stronger records.
- A high confidence source does not automatically make a high confidence interpretation. For example, the existence of the football matches is high confidence; the proposition that they caused the war is not.
