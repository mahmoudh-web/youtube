## Classification key

- **Fact** — supported by a primary or official record.
- **Attributed position** — what a government, military, archive or participant says.
- **Allegation** — a claim of responsibility or motive not established by the located evidence.
- **Interpretation** — an analytical conclusion drawn from several claims.
- **Uncertainty** — unresolved discrepancy or missing evidence.
