## Decision-chain reconstruction

The best-supported chain is: Topsy becomes a local Luna Park liability after reported incidents → Luna Park publicises a planned death → a hanging plan is challenged by the SPCA/ASPCA according to later institutional accounts → Luna Park arranges a combined method with local electric-utility assistance → Edison Manufacturing films the event and copyright records attach the Edison name → later retellings collapse utility, film company and inventor into a single anti-AC actor.

The chain does **not** establish that Thomas Edison selected Topsy, ordered the power connection, attended Luna Park, directed the camera crew or intended the film as an AC/DC demonstration.
