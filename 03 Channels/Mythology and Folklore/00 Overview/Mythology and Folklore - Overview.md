# Mythology and Folklore

**Status:** Unvalidated hypothesis

## Provisional promise

Global myths, legends, and folklore explained through their origins, variations, and cultural meaning.

## Failure points

- Folklore may be presented as historical fact.
- Cultural context may be flattened or misrepresented.
- Source quality is mixed across languages and traditions.
- The channel may drift into generic mythology summaries.
