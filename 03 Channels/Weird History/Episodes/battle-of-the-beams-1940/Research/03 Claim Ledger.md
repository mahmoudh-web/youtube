## Claims explicitly not cleared

- “Britain bent the beams” as a literal, routine technical action.
- “Aspirin/Bromide stopped the Coventry raid” or “Bromide saved Britain.”
- A single precise accuracy figure for Knickebein or X-Gerät without checking the technical source and defining beam alignment versus bomb impact.
- “The RAF used radar to find the beams.”
- Any claim that Churchill, Jones, No. 80 Wing or one individual alone won the contest.
