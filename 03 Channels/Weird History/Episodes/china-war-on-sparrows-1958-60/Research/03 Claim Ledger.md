## Claims currently blocked from narration

- “The sparrow campaign caused the famine.”
- “The campaign killed [a precise national number] sparrows.”
- “All sparrows were wiped out across China.”
- “Scientists unanimously warned Mao and were ignored.”
- “The famine killed [one settled number] people because of this policy.”
- “The 1960 reversal immediately restored the ecosystem.”

Each requires additional primary data or a more carefully bounded formulation.
