## Decision-chain reconstruction

The strongest current sequence is: BSES research interest after Bell’s 1932 Puerto Rico exposure → Mungomery’s Hawaii study and June 1935 import → BSES quarantine breeding at Meringa → August 1935 Gordonvale release → period caveats and Froggatt objection → Commonwealth prohibition → Queensland industry/official lobbying → September 1936 reversal. This chain identifies the operational institution and named actors, but not a complete formal approval trail.

## Date/count controls

- Use “about 100” or “100/101 in differing records,” not a falsely exact count.
- Use “June 1935 arrival” and “August 1935 release” unless the primary quarantine and release logs establish a day.
- Keep the initial release and the later post-ban distribution separate.
