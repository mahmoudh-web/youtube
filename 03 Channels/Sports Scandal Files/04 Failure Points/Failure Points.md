# Sports Scandal Files — Failure Points

- The channel may depend too heavily on current events and lose evergreen value.
- Sports footage may be difficult or expensive to license.
- The audience may fragment across sports.
- Allegations, investigations, sanctions, and final findings may be conflated.
- The topic may duplicate Corporate Case Files or False Verdicts.

## Control

Define boundaries, track rights before scripting, separate allegation from finding, and test multiple sports before narrowing.
