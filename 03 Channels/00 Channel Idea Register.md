## Capture rules

1. Record the idea before evaluating it.
2. Preserve the original wording where possible.
3. Record overlaps instead of deleting duplicates.
4. Add likely failure points alongside the idea.
5. Do not create a dedicated channel folder until the idea is recorded here.
